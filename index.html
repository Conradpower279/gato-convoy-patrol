Patrol
Complete the current mission, then stay in the southern exit with no enemy contact for 15 seconds. An early return lets you refit and retry. Primary objectives earn 1 upgrade point; patrols 2–4 offer optional bonus points. Spend points at port; each upgrade has two levels.

Survival
Deep water reduces detection. Slow down to listen and break contact. Torpedoes launch at 0–15 m. Battery charges surfaced; high underwater speed drains it faster. At zero battery, emergency surfacing starts.

Tactical controls
Observe targets for better lead estimates. G cycles observed contacts; aim manually at the lead diamond. Maneuvers and lost contact reduce confidence. V selects single or three-torpedo spreads. Silent running pauses reloads. Decoys may distract distant escorts; they are ineffective at close range. Time compression stops near threats and objectives.

Weapons
3-inch: ship hulls. 40 mm: aircraft and small craft. 20 mm: close aircraft defense. Mounts reload independently. Shared aim remains relative to your hull.

Arcade simulation. Distances and speeds use game units, not real-world performance.
