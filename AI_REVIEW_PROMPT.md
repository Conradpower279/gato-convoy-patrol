## Design context and preferences

This is an arcade submarine game, not an attempt at engineering-grade simulation. I want straightforward solutions and meaningful tactical choices. The desired loop is: choose an approach at port, observe and attack shipping, evade escorts, complete a distinct mission, return, and spend limited upgrade points. Escaping successfully should matter as much as sinking everything.

Preserve these deliberate controls and behaviors unless you explain a concrete reason to change them:

- All weapons share one player-trained aim angle relative to the submarine's heading.
- Torpedoes are always on Space / LT. F / RT fires the selected gun. Switching weapons cycles only the three guns.
- Empty guns reload automatically, and gun mounts reload independently.
- New games begin at a menu/port flow, never by automatically loading a saved patrol.
- Keep keyboard and standard Xbox-style gamepad support. All in-game menus now support controller navigation; external browser/OS file dialogs may still require mouse/keyboard.
- Keep the game usable offline and preserve the single-file build. Do not introduce a backend, paid service, or framework just for architectural neatness.
- Preserve existing saves through explicit migrations when changing the save format.
